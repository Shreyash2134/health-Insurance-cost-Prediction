The proposed system has significant scope for further enhancement and expansion. In the future, the remaining modules such as direct payment integration for purchasing policies, complete user profile management, and full medical document handling will be implemented to achieve complete system functionality.

Advanced features such as AI-based health recommendation systems can be introduced to suggest specific insurance plans and preventive care routines based on user medical history and predicted risks, improving personalization. Additionally, real-time chat support, telemedicine portals, and virtual health consultations can be integrated to enhance interaction between users and insurance providers.

The system can also be improved by adding smart wearable device support, allowing the application to securely collect daily health metrics like physical activity without continuous manual data entry. Personal health tracking dashboards and policy renewal notification systems can be included for better risk management and user engagement.

Further improvements in mobile application optimization and cloud server scalability will ensure smooth performance across different devices, even during massive enterprise data uploads. With these enhancements, the application can evolve into a complete and advanced insurtech ecosystem.
